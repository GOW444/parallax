A straight-line OLS of energy on GDP per person is heteroscedastic (Breusch-Pagan p = 0.001, R² = 0.72), and its 4 largest residuals are small-population economies (all under 6 M people): Iceland (0.4 M people), Trinidad and Tobago (1.5 M people), Bahrain (1.5 M people), Singapore (5.8 M people). The smallest cluster (C8, high Solar share, n = 8: Cook Islands; Honduras; Namibia; Palestine; Samoa; Tonga; Vanuatu; Yemen) is split off by a share with a small spread (sd 4.1 pp, centre 17.3%), although 88% of its grids are mostly Oil: z-scoring inflates small absolute differences in minor sources. Cluster C6 (high Energy per person, n = 10) is defined by scale, not by the mix: it puts Iceland (28 gCO2/kWh) with Brunei (500 gCO2/kWh). PC1 (23% of variance) is driven by Oil share, Carbon intensity, Hydro share. Raw shares in Euclidean space therefore group countries by their largest source and by scale, not by how their mix is changing.
